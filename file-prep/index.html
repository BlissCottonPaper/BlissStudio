File Prep

Clean up artwork before you print or send it out for a die. Drop the background to transparent, or flatten everything to solid black for foil, white ink, or a letterpress die (like Boxcar Press).

Upload artwork

PNG or JPG. For a die, start with the highest-resolution, highest-contrast version you have.

Remove background

Keys out the background color (sampled from the top-left corner of your image) plus any near-white pixels, so the art sits cleanly on paper.

Flatten to black

Turns every visible pixel solid black on a transparent background — no CMY values, exactly what a foil, white-ink, or letterpress die needs. Use after removing the background.

Hard edgesRecommended for dies — avoids soft, partial-black pixels that can confuse a die cutter.
