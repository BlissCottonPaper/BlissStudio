File Prep for Foil, Letterpress & Die Cutting

Clean up artwork before you print or send it out for a die. Drop the background to transparent, flatten everything to solid black for foil, white ink, or a letterpress die (like Boxcar Press), and vectorize to SVG when a die maker needs paths instead of pixels.

Upload artwork

PNG, JPG, or PDF. For a die, start with the highest-resolution, highest-contrast version you have — a vector PDF is ideal. Multi-page PDFs preview page 1; the same settings apply to every page when you download all.

Click to upload or drag a file here

Remove background

Keys out the background color (sampled from the top-left corner of your image) plus any near-white pixels, so the art sits cleanly on paper.

Remove backgroundOff leaves your image exactly as uploaded.

Flatten to black

Turns every visible pixel solid black on a transparent background — no CMY values, exactly what a foil, white-ink, or letterpress die needs. Use after removing the background.

Flatten to blackOff keeps original colors.
200
Hard edgesRecommended for dies — avoids soft, partial-black pixels that can confuse a die cutter.
Sending this to a die maker (Boxcar Press or similar)? Flatten to black first, then download — a die can be cut from a true 600–1200 DPI bitmap or from vector art. Bold, simple shapes (monograms, gift tags, ex libris marks) trace to clean vector paths below; fine detail is safer left as a high-DPI bitmap.

Vectorize

Traces the flattened black shape into SVG paths — useful when a die maker wants vector art instead of a bitmap. Works best on bold, simple shapes: monograms, gift tags, ex libris marks, small icons. Turn on Flatten to Black first.

Turn on Flatten to Black to enable vectorizing.

Download

JPG can't hold transparency — turn off Remove Background to enable a JPG download.
